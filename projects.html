Machine learning

Loan Default Risk Model: Heritage Credit Union

Built a cost-sensitive binary classification model to assess loan default risk from historical borrower data. Ran EDA and feature engineering to find the key risk drivers, then trained and compared logistic regression, decision trees, random forest and gradient boosting with cross-validation. Tuned the classification threshold on business cost trade-offs (false negatives vs. false positives) to minimize total financial loss and fit underwriting capacity.

Projected to cut annual default losses by about $450K
